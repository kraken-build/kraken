This file contains a list of all pending deprecations. A future version should take care to remove these
deprecated elements.

Deprecated in v0.46.0:

    * The `mkdocs.build` and `mkdocs.serve` tasks are deprecated in favor of just `mkdocs`

Deprecated in v0.45.0:

    * The following `kraken` CLI options and their respective functionality:

        * `--save`, `--no-save`
        * `--resume`
        * `--restart`

Deprecated in v0.37.1:

    * `gitignore(gitignore_io_tokens, gitignore_io_allow_http_request_backfill)` parameters
    * The `kraken.std.gitignore_io` module

Deprecated in v0.26.0:

    * `Task.outputs`
